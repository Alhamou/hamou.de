Shfli: a Syrian online marketplace for cars, real estate, auctions, bazaars and jobs

Shfli is an online marketplace for Syria that brings buyers and sellers together in one place: cars, real estate, live auctions, bazaars, jobs and more. Emad Alhamou was its technical lead and co-founder, and built the backend systems behind the platform and the app.

What is Shfli?

Shfli (شفلي in Arabic) is an online marketplace and app for Syria. It connects sellers and buyers in one place, across cars, real estate, live auctions, bazaars and jobs. On its website, Shfli describes itself as the first platform for buying and selling in Syria.

It combines classified ads and e-commerce with live auctions in one interactive marketplace, so buying, selling and finding work all start from the same place.

What you can find on Shfli

Shfli groups its offers into sections: cars, real estate, live auctions, bazaars, jobs, electronics and more. It is an all-in-one platform for buying, selling and auctions, bringing deals and opportunities into one place.

Built for the Syrian market

  • A marketplace with many categories: one platform for different kinds of deals, with live auctions and bazaars alongside the classified listings.
  • Local maps and a tailored experience: maps and routing tuned to the Syrian market, and an experience designed for phones as well as the web.
  • Fast cloud infrastructure and advanced APIs: the cloud infrastructure and the REST APIs were designed for very fast performance and 99.9% uptime.

Who is behind Shfli

Emad Alhamou, the author of this site, was Shfli's technical lead and co-founder. From February 2025 to April 2026 he led the technical team and built the backend systems for the platform and the app.
